# SQVP International Regulatory & Space Compliance Matrix

This document establishes the strategic compliance and risk-mitigation framework for the SQVP satellite constellation and its integrated quantum computing architectures. All operations are designed to adhere strictly to international space laws, orbital safety treaties, and localized data privacy mandates.

---

## 🛰️ 1. Orbital Safety & Debris Mitigation (UN & FCC Standards)

SQVP is fully committed to preserving the Low Earth Orbit (LEO) ecosystem. Spacecraft designs incorporate active end-of-life disposal mechanisms to strictly prevent long-term orbital contamination.

*   **UN Outer Space Treaty Compliance:** Operates in full alignment with the United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs (UNOOSA) space debris mitigation guidelines.
*   **Active De-Orbiting Profile:** Post-mission (estimated 5-year operational lifecycle), each 6U CubeSat utilizes its onboard propulsion array to lower its perigee into a trajectory designed for complete atmospheric destructive re-entry within 24 months.
*   **Space Situational Awareness (SSA):** Automated orbit tracking profiles are mapped into JSON structures to sync telemetry data with global tracking authorities (such as USSPACECOM) to execute proactive collision-avoidance maneuvers.

## 📡 2. Electromagnetic Spectrum & Optical Frequency Allocation (ITU)

Because SQVP relies on high-speed data delivery channels, communication links are strictly designed to eliminate interference with legacy deep-space networks.

*   **Optical Cross-Links (Laser):** By using 1550 nm Free-Space Optical Communications (FSOC) for inter-satellite and ground links, SQVP operates completely outside the heavily saturated and highly restricted radio frequency (RF) spectrum. This eliminates the requirement for complex International Telecommunication Union (ITU) RF licensing overheads.
*   **RF Telemetry Backup:** Emergency tracking and telemetry channels utilize standard ultra-high frequency (UHF) industrial bands, configured precisely according to national spectrum allocations (e.g., FCC, CEPT).

## 🔒 3. Sovereign Data Governance & Quantum Encryption Supremacy

Legacy terrestrial data centers are vulnerable to cross-border data seizures. Moving database indexing to international space trajectories introduces an unprecedented tier of digital sovereignty.

*   **Geopolitical Data Invariant:** Since orbital video processing nodes operate inside constantly moving LEO orbits outside state borders, raw data asset streams exist in international airspace, secured via space-based encryption.
*   **GDPR & Sovereign Compliance:** By using Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) between passing satellites and local country ground stations, user video databases remain localized inside specific regions. This meets strict terrestrial requirements like Europe's GDPR and America's CCPA without impacting cross-border distribution performance.
